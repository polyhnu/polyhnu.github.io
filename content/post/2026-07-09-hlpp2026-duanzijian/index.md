Zijian Duan attended the [19th International Symposium on High-level Parallel Programming and Applications (HLPP 2026)](https://sites.google.com/view/hlpp-2026), held in Paris, France, on July 9–10, 2026. He presented the paper *“Compiler-Guided Polynomial-Level Parallelism for FHE-Encrypted Machine Learning Inference”* during Technical Session 1.

Congratulations to Zijian on successfully presenting this work and sharing our latest research with the international high-performance computing community.
